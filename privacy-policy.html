Application Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Version: 1.0.0

01 Zero Data Collection

Caveau is a 100% offline, private password and secrets manager built on a Zero-Knowledge architecture.

Our privacy policy is simple and absolute: we do not collect, track, store, or share any personal data or usage metrics.

  • No Remote Servers: Caveau does not operate any cloud servers, databases, or backend infrastructure. All data resides exclusively on your device.
  • No Internet Permission: The application is compiled without network permissions (android.permission.INTERNET), making it technically impossible for the app to communicate externally.
  • No Telemetry or Ads: Zero behavioral tracking, no third-party crash reporters, and zero advertisements.

02 Hardware-Backed Cryptography & Security

All credentials, notes, payment cards, identities, and API tokens stored in Caveau are encrypted locally using the highest cryptographic standards provided by your operating system:

Hardware Keystore

Hardware-backed AES-256 encryption isolated within the device's secure element / Secure Enclave.

Hybrid Authentication

Biometric access (Face ID, Fingerprint) with fallback to a Master PIN protected by iterative cryptographic hashing (200,000 rounds SHA-256) and a 256-bit salt.

Biometric Privacy: Caveau never has access to, collects, or stores your raw biometric data (such as fingerprint scans or facial geometry). Biometric authentication is handled exclusively by your device's operating system (e.g., Android BiometricPrompt, iOS Secure Enclave), which only confirms authentication success or failure.

03 Backups, Data Loss & Limitation of Liability

Because Caveau is strictly offline and operates without central servers:

No Password Recovery Available

You are the sole and exclusive custodian of your Master PIN and decryption keys. The developer does not hold copies of your keys and there is no backdoor, recovery mechanism, or customer support to reset a forgotten Master PIN.

Vital Importance of Exported Backups

In case of app uninstallation, device wipe, loss, or hardware failure, your stored data will be permanently and irretrievably lost if you have not exported a backup.

How Backups Work in Caveau:
Caveau allows you to export an encrypted backup archive (.json) at any time. This file is fully encrypted with a password of your choice and verified with a cryptographic SHA-256 checksum. We strongly recommend saving this backup file in a secure external location (e.g., USB drive, personal cloud, or Google Drive).

Limitation of Liability:
The application is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied. The developer expressly disclaims any liability for any data loss, corruption, or unavailability resulting from forgotten Master PINs, hardware failure, lack of user backups, or operating system tampering (e.g., root/jailbreak).

04 Open Source Transparency

We firmly believe that cybersecurity must never rely on obscurity (Kerckhoffs's Principle). To ensure absolute transparency and allow anyone to audit the code for the absence of backdoors or telemetry, Caveau's complete source code is published as an Open Source project.
